= Adaptive layout
Paul Snow
:revnumber: 0.0.0

Layouts are driven by window width, never by operating system checks. Width classes are defined in `apps/finance_app/lib/app/breakpoints.dart`.

== Breakpoints

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Class | Width (logical pixels) | Navigation

| Compact | Below 600 | Bottom navigation bar with the primary destinations, an add button and a "Scan receipt" button
| Medium | 600 to 1023 | Navigation rail with labels for the primary destinations
| Expanded | 1024 or above | Sidebar listing all destinations in groups, with the add menu at the top (see <<_sidebar>>)
|===

The breakpoints are design starting points rather than device classifications. Content is tested at the boundary widths.

== Destinations

Primary destinations are Overview, Transactions, Budget, Receipts and Plan. Reports, Accounts and Settings are secondary: reached from Overview on compact widths and from the sidebar on expanded widths. Medium widths show only the primary destinations in the rail.

On the compact bottom navigation bar the Transactions destination is labelled "Entries", because the longer word wraps on a phone. Its page title is still "Transactions".

System back returns to Overview from any other destination, and only leaves the app from Overview. On compact and medium widths, secondary destinations also show a back arrow in the app bar that returns to Overview.

On compact widths the add and scan buttons float over every page. A page's own floating button, such as "Add account", "Add bill or income" or "New goal", sits above them, and scrolling views end clear of them.

A single global add action offers expense, income, transfer and receipt capture.

== Sidebar

At expanded widths the `Sidebar` (`apps/finance_app/lib/app/shell.dart`) shows the Add menu at the top, the destinations in three groups, and Settings at the foot:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Group | Destinations

| Money | Overview, Transactions, Accounts
| Plan | Budget, Plan
| Review | Receipts, Reports
|===

Each destination appears once. A hint (Ctrl 1 to Ctrl 5) is shown beside the five primary destinations. Medium widths keep the navigation rail and compact widths the bottom bar.

== Overview dashboard

The Overview page adapts to the width of its content rather than the window. Below 960 logical pixels (`overviewDashboardWidth` in `apps/finance_app/lib/screens/pages/overview_page.dart`) it is a single column: the "Available until payday" card, figure tiles, Your money, Coming up and Needs attention. Wider content shows more figure tiles (spent this period, and owed on cards and loans) and a two-column dashboard that adds a spending pace chart and recent transactions. The chart compares cumulative posted spending through the budget period with an even daily pace of the period's category budget. It is presentation only: the budget is still evaluated by the budget rules. Needs attention shows at most five insights; when there are more, a "Show all N" action opens a "Needs attention" screen that builds the full list lazily (`ListView.builder`), so a long list never slows the Overview.

Each account in Your money says how far its calculated balance can be trusted: the date of the last entry, the date it was last reconciled and, when a reconciliation has been finished, what the statement said, such as "Bank balance $1,000.00 on 5 Nov 2026". On a card or loan this reads "Bank says $50.00 owed on 1 Nov 2026", or "Bank says $50.00 in credit on 1 Nov 2026". The statement figure is shown beside the calculated balance and never changes it.

== Keyboard shortcuts

`AdaptiveShell.shortcuts` defines these shortcuts (Ctrl key):

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
| Shortcut | Action

| Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+5 | Go to Overview, Transactions, Budget, Receipts, Plan
| Ctrl+N | Add expense
| Ctrl+I | Add income
| Ctrl+T | Add transfer
| Ctrl+Shift+R | Add receipt (scan)
|===

NOTE: The shortcuts are registered in the shell for all widths. The requirements list them as a desktop behaviour.
