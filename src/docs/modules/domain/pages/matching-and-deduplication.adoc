= Matching and deduplication
Paul Snow
:revnumber: 0.0.0

Matching proposes relationships; it never creates them. In this phase nothing links automatically: a person confirms every receipt link and every statement-row match. Duplicate detection only warns and never deletes. The code is in `packages/finance_core/lib/src/documents/matching` and `.../text/normalise.dart`.

== Text normalisation

`normaliseText` lower-cases text, replaces every run of characters other than ASCII `a`-`z` and `0`-`9` with one space and trims. Non-ASCII letters become separators, so `Café` becomes `caf`. The rule is part of the shared fixture contract (`csv.fingerprint`; see xref:architecture:shared-fixtures.adoc[]).

`normaliseMerchant` applies `normaliseText`, then drops noise words and any word containing a digit (store numbers, card digits, dates). Noise words are legal suffixes (`pty`, `ltd`, `limited`, `co`, `inc`, `the`), Australian locations (`au`, `aus`, `australia`, `nsw`, `vic`, `qld`, `wa`, `sa`, `tas`, `act`, `nt`), web words (`www`, `com`, `net`, `org`) and payment words (`visa`, `mastercard`, `purchase`, `eftpos`, `pos`, `card`, `debit`, `credit`, `tap`, `contactless`, `sq`, `sp`, `pp`, `paypal`, `sumup`). If nothing would remain, the plain normalised text is used.

`merchantSimilarity` returns a ranking aid between 0 and 1, never a probability:

* 1 when the normalised names are equal.
* 0.9 when every word of one name appears in the other.
* At least 0.8 when both names start with the same word of four or more letters.
* Otherwise the Dice coefficient of character bigrams.

== Receipt to entry

`rankMatchCandidates` lists entries that may be the purchase on a receipt.

.Eligibility
* The entry is `posted` or `pending` (not a draft or a reversed original), and is not a reversal.
* The direction matches: refund entries for refund receipts, other types otherwise.
* When the receipt has a date, the entry is within `dateWindowDays` (default 3) of it.
* The amount is exactly the receipt total in the same currency, or the payee is at least `minimumMerchantSimilarity` (default 0.5) similar.

.Ordering
. `linkable` entries first: same currency, exact amount, and not already fully covered by another receipt.
. Merchant similarity, highest first.
. Entries on the suggested account.
. Date proximity.
. Journal id, for a stable order.

Each candidate carries plain-language `MatchReason` values with a code: `sameAmount`, `amountMismatch`, `currencyMismatch`, `alreadyLinked`, `merchantMatches`, `merchantSimilar`, `accountHint`, `sameDay`, `dateNear` and `receiptEntry`. Even a `linkable` candidate must be confirmed. `confirmMatch` turns a confirmed candidate into a `ReceiptTransactionLink` and refuses a currency or amount mismatch (a `LinkException`); links and their errors are listed on xref:receipts.adoc[].

== Statement row to existing entry

When a statement is previewed, `proposeSourceMatches` asks whether a row is evidence for an entry that already exists, for example one created from a receipt (acceptance scenario A08: one economic purchase with both sources).

* The entry's amount on the account must equal the row amount exactly, and its date must be within 3 days. A changed amount is never proposed.
* Drafts, reversed originals, reversals and entries already matched to another source record are skipped.
* Receipt-created entries rank first, then the nearest date.
* Confirming adds the bank record as evidence instead of creating a second purchase.

== Pending to posted

`matchPendingToPosted` finds the pending source record that a posted record replaces.

* A provider-supplied `pendingReference` equal to a pending record's reference wins and is authoritative.
* Otherwise a heuristic considers pending records on the same account, not rolled back, with merchant similarity of at least 0.8, a posting date from 0 to 7 days after the pending date, the same currency and direction, and an amount within 20 percent (tips, final fuel amounts).
* Exact amounts rank first, then the nearest date; the other candidates are returned as alternatives.
* A heuristic proposal `requiresReview` when the amount or date changed or alternatives exist, so unrelated entries are never merged silently.

== Duplicate warnings

`DuplicateWarning` has three kinds. They are warnings only: legitimate repeated purchases remain possible and the person decides.

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Kind |Detection

|`identicalFile` |Another asset with the same lower-case SHA-256 (`sha256Hex`).
|`similarImage` |Another receipt's image whose perceptual hash is within a Hamming distance of 10 of 64 bits (configurable). This tolerates recompression and slight crops.
|`economic` |Another receipt with the same normalised merchant, date and total. Receipts missing any of the three are never compared.
|===

The perceptual hash is a difference hash (`differenceHash`) over 8 rows of 9 greyscale values (the app resizes the image to 9 by 8). Bit `row * 8 + col` is 1 when `pixels[row][col] > pixels[row][col + 1]`, written row by row, most significant first, as 16 lower-case hexadecimal digits.

For statement rows, duplicate handling uses fingerprints rather than these warnings; see xref:imports.adoc[].
