= Net worth
Paul Snow
:revnumber: 0.0.0

== Calculation

Net worth is assets less liabilities. It is computed from ledger balances, not from income and expense balances:

* the sum of the ledger balances of every account whose _include in net worth_ flag is set; plus
* the balance of `system:reimbursement-receivable` (money owed to the user).

Because liabilities carry negative ledger balances, the sum is assets less liabilities. The calculation can be taken as of a date and counts only the posted ledger (posted and reversed journals).

== Inclusion flags

Each account has three independent flags: include in budget, include in forecast and include in net worth. Archiving an account preserves its history.
