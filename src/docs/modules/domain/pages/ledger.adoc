= Ledger model
Paul Snow
:revnumber: 0.0.0

The application uses a balanced journal internally, but the interface never requires accounting terminology.

== Journals and postings

* A _posting_ references a ledger account and a signed amount in minor units.
* A _journal_ is a set of postings in one currency that sums to zero.
* *Debit is positive.* Assets and expenses increase with positive amounts. Liabilities, income and equity increase with negative amounts.

`validatePostings` reports these error codes, which are shared with the server through the fixtures:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Code | Meaning

| `EMPTY` | No postings (nothing else is reported)
| `TOO_FEW_POSTINGS` | Fewer than two postings
| `MIXED_CURRENCY` | More than one currency (further checks stop)
| `UNBALANCED` | Postings do not sum to zero
| `ZERO_AMOUNT` | A posting has a zero amount
| `OUT_OF_RANGE` | The total of debits exceeds the maximum magnitude
|===

== Mapping user accounts and categories

User accounts map to asset or liability ledger accounts. Categories map to income or expense ledger accounts. A user account or category shares its identifier with its ledger account, so postings reference user-facing identifiers directly.

[cols="1,1",options="header"]
|===
| User account type | Ledger kind

| Transaction, savings, cash, property, investment, superannuation, other asset | Asset
| Credit card, loan | Liability
|===

Liquid money is transaction, savings and cash accounts.

== Account sharing

Each `Account` has a `sharing` setting, `AccountSharing.private` (the default) or `shared` (P1 shared households). A private account, its journals and its reports stay visible only to the member who created it. The setting is stored in the account's JSON as `sharing` and reads as private when absent, so accounts saved before households existed stay private. It does not change how postings, balances or net worth are computed. How households, membership and visibility work across devices is described in xref:sync:households.adoc[Households].

== System accounts

System accounts have fixed identifiers so every device agrees on them without syncing.

[cols="2,1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Identifier | Kind | Used for

| `system:opening-equity` | Equity | Opening balances
| `system:balance-adjustments` | Equity | Balance adjustments, including confirmed reconciliation adjustments
| `system:transfer-clearing` | Asset | Reserved system account
| `system:reimbursement-receivable` | Asset | Money owed to the user by a third party
|===

== How user entries become postings

`JournalFactory` turns a `TransactionDraft` into a balanced journal. Amounts are positive magnitudes, except for opening balances and adjustments, which are signed. Splits must sum exactly to the entry total.

[cols="1,3,3",options="header"]
|===
| Entry | Postings | Notes

| Expense | Each split: category debit. Account: credit for the total | Categorised with splits; categories must be expense categories
| Income | Account: debit for the total. Each split: category credit | Income categories only
| Transfer | Destination account: debit. Source account: credit | Not income or expense. The two accounts must differ
| Card repayment | A transfer from a bank account (credit) to the card (debit) | Reduces cash and card debt; the purchase is not counted again
| Refund | Account: debit. Each split: expense category credit | Reduces the category on the refund's effective date and links to the original with `refundOf`
| Opening balance | User account against `system:opening-equity` | Signed. Not income. Liabilities are entered as money owed
| Adjustment | User account against `system:balance-adjustments` | Signed. A zero amount is rejected
| Reimbursable expense | `system:reimbursement-receivable`: debit. Account: credit | Records money owed back to the user
| Reimbursement | Account: debit. `system:reimbursement-receivable`: credit | With splits instead, it offsets the expense categories directly
|===

In the entry form, choosing the Refund type offers a "Refund of" list of recent purchases when the caller supplies one. Choosing a purchase sets `refundOf`, fills in the account when it is empty and, when the purchase has a single category, that category. "Not linked" is also allowed. The entry editor screen supplies up to 50 of the most recent posted expenses, newest first, so "Refund of" is offered there whenever the entry type is Refund.

For a signed opening balance or adjustment, the user-facing amount is money held for an asset and money owed for a liability; `toLedgerSign` converts a liability amount into a credit.

== Draft validation

Entry validation reports the first failure, in this order: unknown account (and currency match), then for transfers an unknown destination and same-account, then non-positive amount, then for categorised entries missing splits, an unknown or wrong-kind category, a non-positive split and finally a split mismatch. The codes are `UNKNOWN_ACCOUNT`, `SAME_ACCOUNT`, `NON_POSITIVE_AMOUNT`, `MISSING_SPLITS`, `UNKNOWN_CATEGORY`, `WRONG_CATEGORY_KIND` and `SPLIT_MISMATCH`.

== Balances are derived

Balances are never stored as editable truth. Every figure is recomputed from accepted journals by the projection. See xref:domain:net-worth.adoc[] and xref:domain:journal-status.adoc[].
