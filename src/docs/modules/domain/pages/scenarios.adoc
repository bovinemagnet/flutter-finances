= Acceptance scenarios A01 to A04
Paul Snow
:revnumber: 0.0.0

The ledger-related acceptance scenarios from the product requirements, worked through with postings. Debit is positive and amounts are shown in dollars for readability (stored as minor units). Account names are illustrative.

== A01: card purchase, then card payment

_Required result: expenses are $120; the payment reduces bank cash and card debt without another expense._

.Spend $120 on a card (expense)
[cols="2,1",options="header"]
|===
| Ledger account | Amount

| Groceries (expense) | 120.00
| Credit card (liability) | -120.00
|===

.Pay $120 from the bank account (transfer)
[cols="2,1",options="header"]
|===
| Ledger account | Amount

| Credit card (liability) | 120.00
| Bank (asset) | -120.00
|===

The card ledger balance returns to zero, so no debt is owed; the bank balance falls by $120; expense postings total $120.

== A02: transfer to savings

_Required result: net worth and income are unchanged._

[cols="2,1",options="header"]
|===
| Ledger account | Amount

| Savings (asset) | 500.00
| Transaction account (asset) | -500.00
|===

Both accounts are assets, so net worth is unchanged, and no income or expense account is touched. A savings allocation changes only if the user explicitly assigns it.

== A03: split $103.47 into $80.00 and $23.47

_Required result: the journal balances exactly; $80.00 plus $23.46 is rejected._

[cols="2,1",options="header"]
|===
| Ledger account | Amount

| Category one (expense) | 80.00
| Category two (expense) | 23.47
| Bank (asset) | -103.47
|===

The splits sum to the entry total, so the journal balances to zero. With $23.46 the splits total $103.46, which differs from $103.47 and the draft is rejected with `SPLIT_MISMATCH`.

== A04: expense, then a refund next month

_Required result: net expense is $60 across both months; the original period is not silently rewritten._

.Month 1: $100 expense
[cols="2,1",options="header"]
|===
| Ledger account | Amount

| Category (expense) | 100.00
| Bank (asset) | -100.00
|===

.Month 2: $40 refund, linked to the original with `refundOf`
[cols="2,1",options="header"]
|===
| Ledger account | Amount

| Bank (asset) | 40.00
| Category (expense) | -40.00
|===

Month 1 still shows $100 of expense and month 2 shows -$40, a net of $60. The refund is dated on its own effective date.
